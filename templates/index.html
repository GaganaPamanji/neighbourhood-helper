How It Works

For Recipients

  1. Submit a request through our online portal
  2. Describe the type of assistance needed and preferred times
  3. Get matched with a local volunteer
  4. Receive help and provide feedback afterward

For Volunteers

  1. Sign up and create a profile with your skills and availability
  2. Complete a brief orientation session
  3. Browse requests and choose tasks that interest you
  4. Help your neighbors and track your volunteering hours

Services Offered

Household Tasks

Light cleaning, laundry, or organizing to help maintain a tidy living space.

Errands

Grocery shopping, picking up prescriptions, or post office trips.

Companionship

Friendly visits or phone calls to reduce loneliness and provide social interaction.

Outdoor Work

Lawn care, snow shoveling, or minor repairs around the yard.

Technology Help

Teaching basic computer skills or troubleshooting devices.

Childcare Assistance

Occasional babysitting or homework help for families.

Volunteer Portal

Welcome to the Volunteer Portal

Thank you for volunteering with the Neighbourhood Assistance Program. As a volunteer, you play a crucial role in building a stronger, more supportive community.

View Available Tasks

How Volunteering Works

  1. Browse available tasks on your dashboard
  2. Choose tasks that match your skills and availability
  3. Connect with the neighbour who requested help
  4. Complete the task and mark it as finished
  5. Receive feedback from the neighbour you helped

Volunteer Guidelines

  • Be reliable and communicate promptly
  • Respect privacy and confidentiality
  • Only accept tasks you're comfortable with
  • Follow through on commitments
  • Report any concerns to program administrators

Services You Can Offer

Household Tasks

Help with light cleaning, laundry, organizing, and other household chores.

Errands

Assist with grocery shopping, picking up prescriptions, or post office trips.

Companionship

Provide friendly visits or phone calls to neighbours experiencing loneliness.

Outdoor Work

Help with lawn care, snow shoveling, gardening, or minor repairs.

Technology Help

Assist with basic computer skills, device setup, or troubleshooting.

Childcare

Provide occasional babysitting or help children with homework.

Your Impact

Emotional Support

Your kindness reduces isolation and brings joy to your neighbours.

Practical Help

Your assistance with everyday tasks improves quality of life.

Stronger Community

Your involvement builds relationships and community resilience.
